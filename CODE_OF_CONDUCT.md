# Code of conduct

swingbus follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In practice: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, keep discussion on the work, and do not harass or demean anyone. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

If something goes wrong, contact the maintainer, [@akeanti](https://github.com/akeanti), through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules.
